How to play

Terra en disputa is a turn-based strategy game: you conquer countries with dice, collect cards, negotiate pacts and try to meet your objective before everyone else.

TutorialYour first game against bots, step by step. Create and joinHosting, invitations, bots, language and keyboard shortcuts.

The three modes

Classic50 countries, secret objectives and pacts. For 2 to 6 players. CompetitiveClock, final rounds and scoring. For 4 to 6 people. Retake72 countries, situations, missiles and condominiums. For 2 to 6 players.

In a nutshell

  1. Countries are dealt at random and each player places their first armies.
  2. In each round, everyone reinforces and then everyone attacks.
  3. You attack with dice from your countries into neighbors. If you win, you conquer the country.
  4. If you conquered, you get a card at the end of your turn. Three cards are exchanged for more armies.
  5. Whoever meets their secret objective or holds the countries of the common objective wins.
