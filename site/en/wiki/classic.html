Classic

The original mode: 50 countries across six continents, dice, cards, secret objectives and pacts. For 2 to 6 players, with or without bots.

The board

Every country has an owner and a number of armies. Two countries are neighbors when they share a border or are joined by a dotted line across the sea. You can only attack and regroup between neighbors.

Whoever holds a whole continent gets extra armies in every reinforcement:

Continent Countries Extra armies
North America 10 5
South America 6 3
Europe 9 5
Africa 6 3
Asia 15 7
Oceania 4 2

Setup

Countries are dealt at random, with one army in each. Dice decide who gets any leftover countries, and who plays first.

  • 4 to 6 players: everyone gets a secret objective. Then there are two placement passes: 5 armies first, then 3.
  • 2 or 3 players: there are no secret objectives. Each player places 12 armies (with 2 players) or 8 (with 3) in a single pass.

How the game moves

After setup, each player takes a first turn that is attack only. From then on, every round has two passes:

  1. Reinforcement: everyone, in turn, gets armies and places them.
  2. Attacks: everyone, in turn, attacks, regroups and draws a card.

Each new round starts with the next player.

Reinforcement

On your reinforcement turn you get:

  • Half your countries, rounded down. It is always at least 3 armies.
  • The extra armies of every continent you hold. Those go inside that continent.
  • The armies of a card exchange, if you make one.

You must place all of them on your countries before the turn ends.

Attack

You can attack from a country of yours with at least 2 armies into a neighboring country of another player. The dice work like this:

  • The attacker rolls one die per army in the country, minus one, up to 3 dice.
  • The defender rolls one die per army in the country, up to 3 dice.
  • Dice are compared highest to highest, in pairs. For each pair, the lower roll loses one army. Ties go to the defender.

If the attacked country runs out of armies, you conquer it and move 1 to 3 armies in from the attacking country, always leaving at least one behind. You can attack as many times as you like during your turn.

Regroup

When you finish attacking, you can move armies between neighboring countries of yours. Every country keeps at least one army. Armies that arrived in a country during this regroup cannot move again this turn.

Cards and exchanges

If you conquered at least one country this turn, you can draw a card at the end. After your third exchange you need two conquests per turn to get it. You can hold up to 5 cards.

Each card shows a country and a symbol: balloon, galleon or cannon. There are also two wildcards, which count as any symbol. If you own the card's country, you can claim 2 armies there, once per card.

During reinforcement you can exchange three cards with the same symbol, or with all three different symbols. Each exchange is worth more than the last:

Exchange 1st 2nd 3rd 4th 5th After that
Armies 4 7 10 15 20 5 more each time

Whoever eliminates another player takes their cards. If that leaves them with more than 5, they must exchange in their next reinforcement.

Objectives

With 4 to 6 players, everyone has a secret objective. Some are about occupation, such as holding certain continents or a number of countries in each, and others ask you to eliminate a color. There is also the common objective: hold 30 countries. The first player to meet their own objective or the common one wins.

If you must eliminate a color and someone else eliminates it first, your objective becomes the common one of 30 countries.

With 2 or 3 players, whoever conquers all 50 countries wins.

Your objective is always in the Objective panel (key O).

Pacts

On your attack turn you can offer another player a pact from the Pacts panel (key P). The pact starts when they accept it, and the game blocks the attacks it forbids.

  • General non-aggression: neither of you can attack the other.
  • Border non-aggression: neither of you can attack across one border between two countries, one of each player.
  • International zone: a country of yours with a single army that borders both of you. You take turns holding it: whoever has it does not reinforce it, and the other must conquer it on their turn, moving in a single army.

To leave a pact you announce the break. The pact stays in force until every other player has played their attack turn.

Classic Express

In the waiting room the host can choose the Express duration, which puts the game on a clock:

  • Game time: 40 minutes with 2 or 3 players, 60 with 4 and 80 with 5 or 6.
  • Each turn lasts 2 minutes.
  • When the time is up, the current round ends and two final rounds are played, with 3-minute turns.
  • If nobody has met an objective by the end, whoever holds the most countries wins. Ties are broken by armies, then exchanges and finally cards.
  • If your turn runs out, the game places your pending armies at random and moves on.

If someone disconnects, the game waits 5 minutes for them. After that their seat is absent and, if the host allows it, a bot plays for them. Games with no time limit have no clock.
