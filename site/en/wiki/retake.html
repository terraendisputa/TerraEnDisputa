Retake

The biggest mode: 72 countries across seven continents, a new situation card every round, missiles, alliances to attack together and shared countries. For 2 to 6 players, with or without bots.

The basics come from Classic: reinforce, attack with dice, regroup, draw cards and exchange them. This page explains what changes.

The board

There are 16 islands, which count for some objectives. A country can have two owners at once: that is a condominium.

Setup

  1. Countries are dealt at random, with one army in each. Dice assign the leftovers.
  2. Initial placement: two passes, of 8 and 4 armies. With two players it is a single pass of 18.
  3. The first situation card is revealed. If it is Extra reinforcements, everyone places that extra before going on.
  4. First hostility: each player takes one attack-only turn, without missiles.

Then full turns begin.

Rounds and situations

Each round starts with a situation card that applies to the whole round. Then everyone reinforces in turn, and after that everyone plays their attack turn.

Situation Effect
Classic combat The round is played with the normal rules.
Snow The defender adds one die, up to a maximum of 4.
Tailwind The attacker adds one die, up to a maximum of 4.
Crisis Each player rolls a die. The lowest roll gets no card this round.
Extra reinforcements Before reinforcement, each player places extra armies equal to half their countries.
Open borders Attacks are only allowed between countries on different continents.
Closed borders Attacks are only allowed between countries on the same continent.
A color rests That color cannot attack or regroup this round.

To see the round's situation, tap its indicator or press S.

Reinforcement and blockade

You get half your countries (at least 4) plus the extras of every continent where you are in every country, even if you share some of them.

A country surrounded by a single rival is blockaded: that happens when it has at least 3 neighbors and all of them belong to that rival, with 2 or more armies each. A blockaded country cannot receive armies or regroups. If it is your last country, it does not count as blockaded.

Attack

Dice work as in Classic, with one addition: if you have at least twice the defender's armies and they have 3 or more, you roll 4 dice. When you conquer a country you keep the missiles that were in it.

Missiles

  • During reinforcement you can turn 6 armies into 1 missile. At least one army must stay in the country.
  • A missile reaches countries 1, 2 or 3 steps away, along the shortest path. It removes 3, 2 or 1 armies depending on the distance.
  • To fire, you need more missiles in your country than the defender has in theirs.
  • A missile never empties a country: the defender must have more armies than the damage.
  • Missiles can be regrouped like armies.
  • There are no missiles during setup or the first hostility.

Press M to open the missile shot.

Cards and exchanges

At the end of your turn you get a card if you conquered one country, or two countries once you have made three exchanges. Two conquests made attacking together with an ally also count. You can hold up to 5 cards.

  • If you own the country of a card you drew, you can claim 3 armies there. Inherited cards give no bonus.
  • The symbols are plane, ship and tank. The soldier and the wildcard count as any symbol. The supercard is exchanged on its own.
  • If you hold a whole continent without sharing any country, you can take its continent card. Asia, Europe and North America are exchanged on their own, and the others count as one or two symbols. You lose it if you lose the continent, and each player can use each continent card only once.
  • You make at most one exchange per round, during reinforcement or when closing your turn.

Pacts

Pacts are offered from the Pacts panel and start when the other player accepts. The game blocks the attacks and shots a pact forbids.

Pact What it protects
Between countries One country of each, 3 steps apart or less. You do not attack each other there, and no missile crosses the path between them.
Within a continent You do not attack each other inside a continent where you both are.
Between borders You do not attack each other between two continents where you have neighboring countries.
Worldwide You do not attack each other anywhere.
International zone A country of yours with one army and no missiles, bordering both of you. Both of you must have made your third exchange.

In an international zone you take turns holding the country. If on your turn you could conquer it and did not, or you left it with more than one army, you lose a random card and your partner decides whether the zone goes on. Each player can have only one zone.

To leave a pact you break it: it stays in force until the other player finishes one more turn.

Attack together

Two players can join forces to attack a third player's country. Each picks a country of their own that borders the target and has at least 2 armies. The other player accepts, the one who proposed it starts it on their attack turn, and both take turns rolling the dice.

If they conquer it, both move in, with up to 2 armies each, and the country becomes a condominium. Until the ally's turn in the next round, the two origin countries are protected from each other.

Condominiums

A condominium is a country with two owners. The partners cannot attack each other inside it, and each counts the country as their own.

  • To attack a condominium with dice, you must first attack the occupant with more armies.
  • If you drive out one of the two, you can offer a condominium to the one left. If they do not accept, you can keep attacking them from inside or withdraw and leave them the country.

Objectives and victory

The common objective is to hold 45 countries. Each player also has a secret objective, of occupation or of elimination.

  • With 2 players, each has two secret objectives and must meet both.
  • With 3 players, occupation objectives also require 10 countries beyond those of the objective.
  • With 2 or 3 players there are no elimination objectives and no 35-country objective.
  • If you must eliminate someone and another player does it first, you need 45 countries instead.

Retake Express

With the Express duration, the game lasts 40 minutes with 2 or 3 players, 60 with 4 and 80 with 5 or 6. Then two final rounds are played. Setup turns last 2 minutes, full turns 3 and final round turns 4.

If time runs out, whoever holds the most countries wins, with the same tiebreak as Classic: armies, exchanges and cards. If someone disconnects, the game waits 5 minutes and then a bot can play for them.
