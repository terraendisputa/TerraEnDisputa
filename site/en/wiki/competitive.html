Competitive

Classic built for tournaments: games on a clock, automatic final rounds, scoring and the Superliga rules. For 4 to 6 people.

Anything not mentioned here works as in Classic: the board, reinforcement, dice, cards and objectives.

Tables

  • 4, 5 or 6 people.
  • 6: 4 players + 2 bots. This is the Superliga table: four people wait and, when the game starts, two bots take the free seats.

With four people (with or without the two bots) there are no pacts and no elimination objectives.

Setup

Countries are dealt at random, and every player gets countries on at least two continents. Then there are two placement passes of 4 armies each. During the whole setup, each player can add at most 4 armies per continent.

Clock and final rounds

  • Each turn lasts 2 minutes.
  • Game time: 60 minutes with 4 people and 80 with 5 or 6. With 5 or 6 you can choose Express, which lasts 60 minutes. The table of 4 players and 2 bots always lasts 80 minutes.
  • When the time is up, the current round is completed and two final rounds are played, with 3-minute turns.
  • If your turn runs out, your pending armies are placed at random and the turn moves on.
  • Pauses and time with the server off do not count. The host can pause the game.

Rules of its own

  • No suicide attacks: you cannot attack from your only country if it has 4 armies or fewer.
  • Inherited cards: when you eliminate someone you take their cards up to 5. If they had more, you choose which.
  • Pacts (only with 5 or 6): border pacts only, and one per pair of players. You offer them on your attack turn. If the other player rejects it, you wait 5 minutes of game time before offering them again. A broken pact stays in force until the other player has made two more reinforcements. Pacts cannot be broken in the final rounds.

Disconnections and bots

If someone disconnects, the game waits up to 7 minutes each time: 5 minutes with the clock stopped and then the 2 minutes of their turn. If they do not come back, a bot plays for them and their pacts end. The host can turn off bot replacement.

From the second-to-last round, a player left with a single country can press Ask for a bot so a bot plays for them until the end. The points are still theirs.

For qualifiers, semifinals and finals there is the Deciding game, which the host checks before starting. It uses no bots: whoever goes absent becomes inactive, and their turns last 1 minute without waiting for them.

End of the game and scoring

You win as in Classic: by meeting your secret objective or holding 30 countries. If the final rounds end and nobody has won, the table ranks the players still in the game by countries, then armies, exchanges and cards. Eliminated players come after them, in reverse order of elimination.

Item Points
Final position, 1st to 6th 25, 18, 15, 12, 10 and 8
Winning the game +25
Each player you eliminated +10
Being eliminated −5

Whoever wins by eliminating the color of their objective does not get the 10 points for that elimination. If two players are tied on everything, they share the points of their positions. The score is official only if at least 6 rounds were completed.
