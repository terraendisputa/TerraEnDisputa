Create and join a game

One person hosts the game on their computer and creates the table. Everyone else joins from the browser with a link, with nothing to install and no accounts.

The host

To host, download the package for your system from Releases, extract the folder and open ted.cmd on Windows or ./ted on Linux. The host panel opens. To play remotely or keep it always on a server, follow the installation guide.

Games are created from the host panel:

  1. Choose the mode and the number of players, and press Create game.
  2. Choose the network your friends use.
  3. Set the required players, bots and duration. The options depend on the mode.
  4. Copy the invitation link and send it to the others. If you play too, press I will play too.
  5. When everyone is ready, press Start game.
Mode Players Duration
Classic 2 to 6, with bots if you like No limit or Express
Competitive 4 to 6, or 4 people and 2 bots Always on a clock
Retake 2 to 6, with bots if you like No limit or Express

The players

  1. Open the invitation link.
  2. Choose a nickname and a color, and press Join.
  3. In the waiting room, press I'm ready.

Your seat is saved in the browser. If you close it or lose the connection, open the link again in the same browser to keep playing.

Bots

Bots play by the same rules as people and come in three levels: Easy, Normal and Hard. The host manages them like this:

  • Bots: in the waiting room, choose how many seats bots take. There must always be at least one person.
  • Add bot and Remove bot: during the game, a bot can take the place of someone who left, and the host can give the place back.
  • Replace absent players with a bot: in games on a clock, a bot plays for anyone who disconnects and does not come back in time.

In Classic and Retake, bots also offer and accept pacts.

Language, theme and text size

  • Choose the language at the bottom of every screen: Español or English.
  • The sun or moon button switches between the dark and light themes.
  • The Aa button makes text and menus larger.
  • In the game there are buttons for the music and for the sound that marks your turn.

Each preference is saved in your browser.

Keyboard shortcuts

Key Action
Enter Confirms the open menu: attack, place, move.
Esc Cancels the selection, then closes menus.
↑ ↓ Change the chosen country in the menu.
← → Change the number of armies.
Del ⌫ Cancel the selection.
+ − Zoom the map in or out.
O C P Open the objective, cards and pacts.
J Shows the next player.
S M In Retake: the round's situation and the missile.
